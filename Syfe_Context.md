## About Syfe

Syfe is a digital wealth management platform regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), holding Capital Markets Services Licence No. CMS100837. The platform serves over 250,000 users across Singapore, Hong Kong, and Australia, offering managed portfolios, brokerage, and cash management — all accessible through a single app. Syfe has been featured in the Financial Times, Bloomberg, TechCrunch, CNBC, and The Straits Times.

The platform is built around three pillars: Invest (managed portfolios), Trade (brokerage), and Save (cash management). Everything is designed for simplicity — no CDP or brokerage account required for managed portfolios, automatic dividend reinvestment, and a straightforward tier-based fee structure.

## Investment Strategy

Syfe's managed portfolio philosophy rests on three principles:

1. **Diversification**: Reduce risk and enhance potential returns by spreading investments across asset classes (equities, bonds, gold), geographies, and sectors. Syfe's view is that diversification is a return driver, not just a defensive choice — a position validated in 2025 when globally diversified portfolios outperformed US-concentrated ones.

2. **Cost-effectiveness**: Portfolios are built using low-cost index ETFs as building blocks. The expense ratios of Syfe Core portfolios range from 0.20–0.27%, compared to 1–2% for actively managed mutual funds. Lower costs compound into meaningfully higher long-term returns.

3. **Long-term focus**: The goal is to build wealth over time. Syfe encourages staying invested and being consistent, regardless of short-term market noise.

### Smart Beta (Factor Investing)

Syfe's Core portfolios use a Smart Beta strategy — a rules-based, systematic approach that sits between pure passive indexing and active stock-picking. The strategy tilts towards three academically-supported factors:

- **Value**: Companies that are undervalued relative to their fundamentals.
- **Size**: Smaller companies that historically offer a risk premium.
- **Quality / Profitability**: Companies with strong returns on capital, stable earnings, and consistent profitability.

In the latest rebalance (late 2025), Syfe strengthened its profitability factor exposure by introducing the Dimensional US High Profitability ETF (DUHP), complementing the existing VanEck Morningstar Wide Moat ETF (MOAT). Academic research shows highly profitable companies tend to perform well even when other factors lag.

### Portfolio Construction Process

1. **Asset allocation**: Each asset is selected based on its risk contribution using an Asset Class Risk Budgeting approach, ensuring optimised allocations.
2. **Factor investing**: The equity component is optimised with Smart Beta factors.
3. **Optimisation**: Portfolios target maximum expected return for each risk level.
4. **Biannual rebalancing**: In April and October, portfolios are rebalanced to address price and factor drifts and maintain strategic asset allocation.

Suitable for investors with a 15+ year horizon who can tolerate significant short-term volatility. SRS-eligible.

**Core Growth**

~70% equities, ~25% bonds, ~5% gold. Uses the same equity ETFs as Equity100 but adds:
- Fixed income ETFs (IUAA, AGGU, BBIN) for bond exposure
- GLDM (SPDR Gold MiniShares) for gold — switched from GLD to GLDM in the latest rebalance, saving 30 basis points in fees

**Core Balanced**

~40% equities, ~49% bonds, ~11% gold. Nearly equal split between equities and bonds. The most balanced portfolio in the Core range — genuine multi-asset diversification. The fixed income allocation is larger, providing more downside protection while still capturing equity upside. Suitable for 3+ year horizon.

**Core Defensive**

~25% equities, ~62% bonds, ~13% gold. Bond-dominated portfolio with equity and gold for modest growth. Contains both GICS equity sectors and fixed income bond-type exposures (treasuries, agency MBS, corporate bonds). Designed for capital preservation and short-term goals (2+ year horizon). The most conservative Core option.

## Recent Developments (2025-2026)

### Core Portfolio Rebalance (Late 2025)

The most significant rebalance in Syfe's history, reflecting a shift in global market dynamics:

**What changed:**

1. **Geographic diversification**: Reduced US concentration, increased developed ex-US exposure (Europe, Japan). Final allocation: ~65% US, ~21% Developed ex-US, ~14% EM. Rationale: after years of US mega-cap dominance, growth leadership is broadening globally.

2. **Factor enhancement**: Added Dimensional US High Profitability ETF (DUHP) for systematic profitability exposure. Trimmed QQQ (Nasdaq 100) to reduce mega-cap tech concentration. Refined value and size factor allocations.

3. **Fixed income adjustments**: Shortened duration to reduce interest rate sensitivity. Reduced US concentration in bonds.

4. **Gold optimisation**: Trimmed gold after strong rally, locking in gains. Switched from GLD to GLDM (SPDR Gold MiniShares), saving 30 basis points (75% fee reduction) while maintaining identical gold exposure.

**Why it matters:**

In 2025, emerging markets led global equities with +34.4% returns (USD), while developed markets outside the US also outperformed. The S&P 500 lagged other major markets for the first time in two decades. This validated Syfe's emphasis on global diversification over US concentration.

### 2026 Market Outlook

Syfe's investment team sees 2026 as a year of transition:

- **From inflation to disinflation**: Inflation is easing, driven by productivity improvements, automation, and cooling wage growth. PCE inflation expected around 2.4–2.5%.
- **From restriction to neutrality**: The Fed is moving toward a neutral rate around 3.1–3.4% by end-2026.
- **From concentration to breadth**: Returns are broadening across regions, sectors, and asset classes. Concentration risk is being exposed while diversified portfolios prove more resilient.

Key investment themes for 2026:
1. **AI**: Still powerful but shifting from narrative to execution. Capital discipline matters more as infrastructure spending reaches ~2% of US GDP.
2. **Diversification as a return driver**: With high dispersion and broadening earnings, allocation and selection matter more than riding a single index.
3. **Emerging Markets and Asia**: Benefiting from weaker USD and improving earnings. China's expanding AI footprint adds to the case.
4. **Bonds and income re-enter the opportunity set**: BBB US corporate yields ~5%, 10Y Treasury ~4.2%. Income is a meaningful contributor to total return, not just defensive.

## Core-Satellite Approach

Syfe positions its product lineup as a core-satellite strategy:

**Core (foundation):** Core Equity100, Core Growth, Core Balanced, or Core Defensive — depending on risk tolerance and time horizon. These provide global diversification, factor-driven returns, and disciplined rebalancing.

**Satellites (complements):**
- Income+ for bond income and stability
- REIT+ for Singapore real estate exposure and dividends
- Select Themes for targeted thematic bets (AI, China, ESG, Healthcare)
- Cash+ for liquidity and short-term needs

The idea is that the core provides the steady foundation while satellites express specific investment views or income needs. This framework is central to how Syfe markets its products and advises clients.
